Support

Email support@bodtea.app and we'll reply within two working days. It helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Where is my data?
On your phone, in an encrypted file. MotherTea works without an account. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I get a copy of my records?
Settings → Export my records.

How do I delete my data?
Settings → Delete all local data removes everything on your phone.

How do I stop MotherTea reading Apple Health?
iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MotherTea.

How do I turn off cycle tracking?
Cycle → Turn off tracking & delete cycle data. Your cycle logs are deleted straight away.

How do I cancel my membership or ask for a refund?
Memberships are handled by Apple. Cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MotherTea. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is MotherTea medical advice?
No. MotherTea gives general information from published guidance. Your midwife, doctor or care team always comes first. If you're worried, use Get help now in the app or call your maternity unit, midwife or GP. In an emergency, call 999 (UK) or 911 (US).

Privacy requests

To access, correct, export or delete your personal data, write to support@bodtea.app. We reply within one month.
