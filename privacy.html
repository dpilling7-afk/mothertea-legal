Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

Who we are: TheAppGroup Ltd, 1 Chislehurst Place, FY8 4RU ("MotherTea", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

Contact: support@bodtea.app. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

The short version

  • Your records stay on your phone. Your pregnancy, fertility, recovery and cycle records, workouts, food, check-ins and progress photos are stored on your device in an encrypted file. You don't need an account to use MotherTea.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you use a feature that needs it: checking your App Store membership, searching for a packaged food or scanning a barcode. Each is explained below.
  • No advertising, no data selling, no tracking. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never use your health data for marketing.
  • MotherTea is for people aged 18 and over.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you enter is saved on your phone in an archive encrypted with AES-GCM. The key is held in your device's secure storage. This includes:

  • your profile and answers from setup: name, year of birth, goals, experience, life stage and health notes, your reasons, and your signature on your day one card
  • your stage and its records: trying and fertility logs (ovulation tests, temperature, tests), treatment cycles and medicines, pregnancy logs (symptoms, baby's movements, appointments, hospital bag and contraction timings), recovery after birth (pelvic floor, mood check-ins, feeding), and anything you record after a loss
  • workouts, sets and personal records
  • meal plans, food logs and allergies
  • water
  • cycle logs and symptoms (only if you turn cycle tracking on)
  • wellbeing check-ins
  • progress photos and measurements
  • reminders and settings

We cannot see this data. It is deleted if you delete the app, or if you choose Settings → Delete all local data.

Widgets. If you turn on MotherTea widgets, a small summary (for example today's session, water or your cycle phase) is shared with the widget through an app group on your device. Cycle widgets are marked sensitive, so iOS hides them while your phone is locked. Widgets are off by default.

Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. Their text is created on your phone.

2. Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, MotherTea reads only the categories you allow, such as workouts, steps, sleep, heart rate, active energy and menstrual flow. MotherTea does not write to Apple Health.

Apple Health data:

  • is used only to show your own training and recovery records inside MotherTea
  • is never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold or shared with data brokers
  • is never shared with third parties
  • stays on your device.

You can withdraw access at any time in the iOS Settings app (Health → Data Access & Devices → MotherTea).

3. Your membership

MotherTea's membership is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details. To know whether your membership is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your App Store purchase receipt and a random app user ID. No name, email or health data is sent to RevenueCat.

4. Food lookups

When you search for a packaged food or scan a barcode, MotherTea sends the search words or barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to fetch nutrition information. No account details or personal data are sent with it. Searches of the built-in UK food table stay on your phone.

5. Speech

If you log a set by voice, iOS speech recognition turns your words into text. Apple may process the audio on its servers, under Apple's privacy policy. Nothing is logged until you review it.

6. Why we use your data (UK GDPR)

  • To provide the app: performance of our contract with you.
  • Health, cycle and life-stage information: these are special-category data. They stay on your device. We only send them off it with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To meet legal obligations, where they apply.

7. Who we share data with

We use these service providers (processors), only for the features described above:

  • RevenueCat checks your App Store membership. It is in the USA.

Data sent to US providers is protected by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as applicable.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

8. How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Membership records: RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide and account for your membership.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and you can object to or restrict processing. You can withdraw consent at any time.

  • In the app: Settings → Export my records gives you a full copy, and you can delete data in the app at any time.
  • By email: write to support@bodtea.app. We reply within one month.

10. Children

MotherTea is not for anyone under 18. Setup asks for year of birth and stops anyone under 18 without saving anything.

11. Health information is not medical advice

MotherTea is not a registered healthcare provider or a medical device, and we are not your care team. What the app shows is general information based on published guidance (NHS, NICE, RCOG, ACOG, CDC, FDA): what we would do, not instructions for you. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition, and due dates, fertile windows and cycle phases are estimates. Your midwife, doctor or care team always comes first. In an emergency, call 999 (UK) or 911 (US). The full wording is in section 2 of our Terms.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
